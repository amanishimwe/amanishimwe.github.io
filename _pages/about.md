Data scientist at the Uganda Bureau of Statistics, working on natural language processing for official statistics. I build language-model systems for statistical classification and retrieval-augmented access to official data, and I study how small language models represent African-language names. I have presented at the UNECE Group of Experts on Consumer Price Indices and the IAOS–ISI Conference.

The full record of roles, projects, teaching, publications, and talks is on my [CV](/cv/).
